How to use Symplyve

Everything you need in five minutes.

Sign in

Click Sign in, pick your name and enter your PIN. The first time, you will be asked to create a 4 to 6 digit PIN. Five wrong tries lock sign-in for 15 minutes.

Apply for leave

Click Apply Leave, or tap a date on the Team Calendar to start with that day filled in. Choose the type and dates, check the day counter, add a reason if you like, then Submit Request. You get a confirmation email.

Track your request

Open My Leave to see your balance and history. A request is Pending until your manager decides, then Approved or Rejected, with an optional note. You are emailed either way.

See who is out

Team Calendar has a month view and a timeline. Faded bars are still pending. Who's Out Today sits on the right.

Ask a question

Open Ask AI and type things like "Who took the most leaves?" or "Who is on leave on Jan 31?".

Sign out when done

On a shared computer, use the sign-out button (top right). Sessions also end by themselves after about 6 hours.

Leave types
AnnualPaid time off. Counts against your yearly balance once approved.
SickIllness or medical appointments.
CasualShort, unplanned personal time.
WFHWorking remotely. Shows on the calendar so the team knows.
UnpaidLeave without pay.
Good to know
  • You cannot request dates that overlap another pending or approved request.
  • Annual requests larger than your remaining balance are refused.
  • Your reason and manager notes are visible only to you and the admin.
  • Forgot your PIN? Ask the admin to reset it. You will then be asked to create a new one.
For admins
  1. Choose Admin in the sign-in box and enter the admin PIN.
  2. Open Approvals and click Review on a request. Add an optional note.
  3. Reject takes effect immediately. Approve asks for a 6-digit code emailed to the admin address, so a stolen session cannot approve leave.
  4. Use Reports for a searchable list of every request.
